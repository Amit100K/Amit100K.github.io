About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate and junior research fellow focused on AI for healthcare. My work covers EEG denoising, neurological disorder diagnosis and biomedical signal analysis, using signal decomposition, time–frequency representations, wavelet scattering and transformers.

I work in MATLAB and Python, and I am interested in intelligent healthcare systems and BCI technology.
